# Project Description

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard for Super Store sales analysis. The report helps understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, shipping modes, payment methods, and state-wise performance.

## Problem Statement

The business needs a clear view of sales and profit performance across different regions, categories, and customer segments. The dashboard is designed to help identify high-performing areas, low-profit segments, and trends over time.

## Business Questions

- What are the total sales, profit, profit margin, quantity, and average delivery days?
- Which customer segment contributes the highest sales?
- Which category and sub-category perform best?
- Which ship mode generates the most sales?
- How do monthly sales and profit compare year over year?
- Which states show strong or weak sales and profit performance?

## Dashboard Pages / Sections

- KPI cards for sales, profit, margin, quantity, and delivery days
- Sales by segment
- Year-over-year monthly sales
- Year-over-year monthly profit
- Sales by ship mode
- Sales by payment method
- Sales by category
- Sales by sub-category
- State-wise sales and profit map

## Skills Demonstrated

- Power Query data cleaning
- Data modeling
- DAX measures
- KPI design
- Time-series analysis
- Interactive filtering
- Dashboard layout and formatting
- Business insight presentation
